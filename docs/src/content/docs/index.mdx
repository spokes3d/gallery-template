---
title: gallery-template
description: Galleries of computational studies, with cached results and honest status boxes.
---

A gallery is a repository of **studies**: complete, standalone scripts that use the codes of a
project, each with its result cached and a status box that says what it verifies and what it
does not. This template is the skeleton of a gallery and the tools that run it:

- `gallery check`: every `study.yml` is complete, and the public studies may go public;
- `gallery build`: the Studies pages of the site, from the cached results (a simulation runs
  only when its result is not cached, or when asked for);
- `gallery export`: the public gallery, the studies marked `public: true`, as a repository;
- a reusable workflow that pushes that export to the public repository as a pull request.

A project keeps its studies in a private gallery, published or not, and publishes some of them
in a public one: verification, method demonstrations and examples at once, novel results once
their preprint is out.
